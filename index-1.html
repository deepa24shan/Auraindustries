Aura Industries is a government-registered, IEC-licensed enterprise authorised to conduct international import and export operations. The company is listed under Gentian Solarcomtech Private Limited's MSME registration covering multiple business units.

Our operations span solar energy equipment manufacturing, precision metal fabrication, telecom infrastructure installation, and international wholesale distribution of agricultural commodities and industrial goods.

We serve buyers and suppliers across India, the European Union, and the United Arab Emirates, with EUR-denominated international payments processed through SEPA banking infrastructure.

International Payments

SEPA-Enabled
EUR Transactions

Aura Industries accepts EUR payments from European buyers through SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) and SEPA Direct Debit (SDD) payment infrastructure, enabling smooth trade settlement with EU/EEA counterparties.

SEPA Credit Transfer

Receive EUR payments directly from EU/EEA buyers via SEPA SCT. Fast, low-cost settlement for export invoices. IBAN-referenced transfers linked to commercial documentation.

SEPA Direct Debit

Pre-authorised EUR collections from European buyers under signed SEPA Core or B2B mandates. Ideal for recurring supply contracts and ongoing wholesale relationships.

USD SWIFT Transfers

Standard SWIFT wire transfers in USD for trade with UAE (Dubai) and other non-SEPA international markets. Full invoice and shipping documentation provided for every transaction.
